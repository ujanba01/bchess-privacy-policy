Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how BCHESS handles information when you use the BCHESS Android chess game. BCHESS offers offline chess against the computer, local two-player chess, and Firebase-powered online multiplayer when online services are configured.

Information stored on your device

Names you enter for local games and the associated win, loss, draw, and score totals are stored on your device. They are not sent to BCHESS servers. They remain on your device until you clear the app's storage or uninstall the app.

Online play and Firebase

If Firebase is configured for the app, BCHESS uses Google Firebase Authentication to create a pseudonymous, anonymous player ID for online play. The app does not ask for your email address, phone number, or password.

When you use online play, BCHESS and its Firebase services process and store the anonymous player ID, game identifier, chess position, moves, whose turn it is, game status and result, and game timestamps. A private rate-limit record is also maintained to prevent excessive game creation. Player names and local scoreboard totals are not included in online game records.

Waiting games are visible to signed-in BCHESS players so they can be joined. Game details are available to the players in that game. Google Firebase processes this information to provide authentication, matchmaking, and game synchronization. See Google Firebase privacy and security information.

Online data is kept in Firebase until it is deleted by the developer or a deletion request is fulfilled. Offline and local two-player modes do not require Firebase online play.

How information is used and shared

Information is used only to save local scores, authenticate anonymous online players, match players, synchronize chess games, and protect the online service from abuse. Firebase, operated by Google, processes online data as a service provider. BCHESS does not sell personal information and does not use advertising or analytics SDKs.

Permissions and security

BCHESS requests internet access for Firebase online play. It does not request access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone, or photos. Online communications use Firebase services over encrypted connections. No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Account and data deletion requests

To request deletion of the anonymous Firebase player account and associated online game data, email ujanba27@gmail.com with the subject “BCHESS data deletion request.” Include any relevant game ID and enough information for us to locate the data. Do not send passwords or other sensitive information. We will use the details only to locate and process your request. Local names and scores can be removed at any time by clearing BCHESS app storage or uninstalling the app.

Children's privacy

BCHESS does not ask users to provide personal contact details. If you believe a child has provided personal information to BCHESS, contact us using the address below so we can review the request.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the app or its data practices change. The latest version will be published at this page, and the “Last updated” date above will be revised.
